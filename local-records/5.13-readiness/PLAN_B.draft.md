# When the Workspace is not available

Draft (English; Arno's copy follows later). For the agents and the manager of the call center. Backlog
CMA-032 ("Plan B for agents if the CMA is down": README Open decisions, line 2100: "today: HubSpot and Aircall
as they work now"). Tone: short, active, calm. Written on 17 October 2026 by the readiness subagent of the
IT audit; the lead reviews and places it.

---

## Keep working as before

The Workspace keeps your hours and shows your team's day. Your customer work was never in it. Calls,
notes, tasks and outcomes live in HubSpot and Aircall, exactly as before the Workspace existed. When the
Workspace is not available, you keep calling and keep logging your calls there. Nothing of that is lost
and nothing of that waits for the Workspace.

## Note your times on paper or in a sheet

The Workspace cannot record your day while it is away. Write your times down yourself, so your manager can
enter them later. One line per day, with these fields:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Date | 25 Oct 2026 |
| Start (clock-in) | 09:02 |
| Each break: start and end | 10:45 to 11:00; 13:00 to 13:30 |
| Each change of status and its time | 14:00 Available - Operations; 15:30 Coaching |
| End (clock-out) | 17:28 |
| Note | "Workspace unavailable"; anything your manager should know |

Use the clock on your desk or phone, write the time as hh:mm, and note every change the moment it happens.
Your manager has a sheet with these columns; ask for it if you do not have one.

## Who collects the sheets

Your manager. Hand your sheet in at the end of the day, or the next morning at the latest. The manager
enters each day in the Workspace once it is back: Team hours → Correct a day (for a day that was partly
recorded) or Add day (for a day that was not recorded at all), with the reason "Workspace unavailable
<date>". The reason is kept with the change, together with the manager's name, so everyone can see later
why the day was entered by hand.

## What happens to a day you could not close

If you were clocked in when the Workspace went away and could not clock out, the Workspace closes that day
by itself later in the evening: at the end of your published shift if you had one, otherwise at 17:00, and
never later than the last change it saw. It marks the day for review and tells you and your manager. Your
manager corrects it from your sheet to your real clock-out. You do not need to do anything.

## Who to tell

Tell your manager as soon as the Workspace does not load for you. The manager tells Martin. One message
per team is enough; the manager keeps the team informed.

## What not to do

- Do not clock in on a colleague's device or browser.
- Do not sign in with someone else's account, and do not let anyone sign in with yours.
- Do not try to catch up your times yourself afterwards: your manager enters them from your sheet.
- Do not stop logging your calls in HubSpot and Aircall: that is where they belong, with or without the
  Workspace.

## How you know it is back

Open the Workspace. It is back when the page loads, shows your name at the top and a short code such as
"Version 1a2b3c4" at the bottom left. Clock in as usual. If you were already clocked in before, check My
day: if the day is still open, carry on; if it was closed, your manager has it on the review list.

## For the manager

- Keep the sheets until the days are entered and checked on Team hours; then follow the usual rule for
  paper with staff times.
- Enter the days with the reason "Workspace unavailable <date>", one correction per day and person.
- Days the Workspace closed by itself show on Team hours as needing correction; correct them from the
  sheets.
- Tell Martin when the Workspace is back for everyone and whether anything is still missing.
