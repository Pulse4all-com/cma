# The Workspace in one page (for agents)

**Open** `workspace.pulse4all.app` in Chrome on your desktop, signed in with your pulse4all.com Google account. Keep the tab open all day; it is your second screen next to HubSpot and Aircall.

**No access yet?** The page shows your account id. Give it to your supervisor; they add you in a minute.

**Start your day:** Welcome → **Clock in**. Your status becomes Available.

**Change status:** My day (key **3**): press **S**, choose with the arrows, **Enter**. Break and Lunch stop the clock; Available starts it again. Choose a pause *before* you walk away.

**Messages (key 2):** your supervisor's messages and, later, new-lead alerts. A badge in the top bar shows how many are unread. A toast appears when something arrives; **Open record** opens the record in a new window. An **urgent** message opens a pop-up with a sound: read it and press **A** (Acknowledge); it cannot be closed any other way. Press the **Desktop notifications** button once in Messages and allow it, so you also see messages when another window is in front.

**My hours (key 4):** your own time per day, with the pauses. Nobody else's.

**My schedule (key 5):** your published shifts.

**End your day:** **Log out** (key **L**). It ends your workday and signs you out. Forgot? The day is closed automatically after midnight and your supervisor corrects the end time.

**Keys:** numbers switch pages, **S** picks a status, **M** marks all messages read, **L** logs out, **Esc** closes a dialog. The keycaps next to buttons show what works.

Customer data never shows in the Workspace: that stays in HubSpot and Aircall.
