# Metric audit, 23 October 2026

Part of the CMA documentation (README.md, Documentation map). The findings of the audit that built
the metric catalog (docs/metrics/catalog.json, PR of this date): everywhere the code, the docs
(features.md, kpis.md, api.md, the data-model files) and the intended definition disagree, or a
figure stands on a prerequisite that is not in place. Every formula was derived from the SQL and
TypeScript as they are, then compared with the docs; the catalog's entries carry the same statuses.

**Nothing in this file is fixed in the catalog PR.** Each finding names who decides; a fix is a
separate brief after review. Severity: high = a wrong number or a wrong source; medium = ambiguous
or inconsistent across pages; low = wording, a missing hint or stale documentation.

Counts: 1 high, 18 medium, 50 low, 69 in all.

### MA-003 · `attempts.median_speed_to_lead`, `attempts.outcomes.median_speed`, `attempts.list.speed_to_lead`

- **What the code does:** Attempts per deal (0008z) measures speed to lead to the first ATTEMPT: under attempts.source='telephony' (Pulse4all's effective value) only an outbound Aircall call on a counted line, currently linked to a CRM call associated with the deal or its contacts, and only inside the window creation−pre_window → earliest(close, first order, now). Both medians are percentile_cont(0.5) over called/attempted deals of a market with office hours, in business minutes - so the units match but the call sets differ: a hand-logged HubSpot call without a telephony link, a call on an uncounted line, or a first Aircall call outside the window moves the two pages apart.

- **Severity:** medium
- **Recommendation:** Document the difference where both pages explain themselves (the hints and features.md §4/§5), and retire or amend the 19 Oct decision row; alternatively make Speed to lead's first call honour attempts.source. Measure in prod how many lead deals have a first outbound CRM call with no telephony link (the page's 'Calls logged without a telephony call' approximates it) before choosing.
